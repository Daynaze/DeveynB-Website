Hello! My name is Deveyn Baptiste, and I`m a Florida-based multidisciplinary artist specializing in Illustration (12 years), Animation (7 years), and Music Composition (5 years).

I`m a creator who`s always learning new skills and exploring new ways to express my creativity. I love the process of bringing an idea to life, often becoming so immersed in a project that it`s all I can think about! My interests extend beyond my main specialties to include Video Filming, Editing, Coding, Game Development, Photography, and Writing.

I especially love animating to music and songs that I can feel the passion from or resonate with, allowing my imagination to run wild and inspiring me to bring the music to life through my art!

If you`re interested in working with me or commissioning me for a project, feel free to check out my VGen! For serious inquiries, collaborations, or any questions, you can also reach me through my email. I look forward to creating with you!
